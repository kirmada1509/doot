# Product Rules

1. The public product is one hotline number, not an app or website.
2. The first spoken lines disclose AI use, recording, purpose, retention, deletion, and emergency boundaries.
3. Travel/local booking, payments, medical dispatch, and caseworker multi-client mode are outside this build.
4. Normal cases interrupt the caller exactly once for a decision.
5. Any exception that could cause an unsafe automatic fallback goes to human review.
